Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Data Collection & Usage

Sirius is a personal media browser designed for high-performance video playback. We do not host or store your media files on our own servers. All content processing is handled directly on your device to ensure maximum privacy and speed. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal usage data or viewing history.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To provide Sirius for free, we use ironSource LevelPlay and Unity Ads mediation. These third-party services may collect technical identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant advertisements. Please refer to ironSource and Unity's respective privacy policies for details on their data processing.
